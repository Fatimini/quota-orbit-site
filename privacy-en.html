Privacy information

For the 1.4.0 store version · Revised September 21, 2026

Quota Orbit is an independent quota viewer developed by 张浩锐, not an official OpenAI product. It retrieves account quota from OpenAI and stores chart history on this Mac. The developer does not operate a server to receive app runtime data and has not integrated advertising into the app.

Sign-in and network connections

Sign-in takes place on OpenAI’s official website. The app does not ask you to enter your account password in its interface. The bundled Codex component receives authorization through a local callback and uses authentication tokens to maintain sign-in, read account status, and retrieve quota. Account information may include an email address, account identifiers, and plan details. Email addresses are masked in the interface.

The component sends authentication information, account identifiers, and client information, such as its version, operating system, and architecture, to OpenAI services. The service also receives the connection’s IP address. Quota requests may include reading quota-reset service status; the app does not purchase or perform quota resets or send model conversations.

The app disables the component’s plugin functionality, analytics events, and outbound log, trace, and metrics telemetry channels. A bundled model catalog avoids online model-list refreshes. These limits do not mean that the app is offline or that no third party processes data: account, authentication, quota, and related service configuration may still require network requests. OpenAI’s processing and retention are governed by its Privacy Policy and your account settings.

Information stored on this Mac

Sign-in state, quota history, and preferences are stored in this app’s own macOS sandbox container. Credentials are stored in local files with restricted file permissions, not in Keychain. Masking the email in the interface does not anonymize the credential file. The app does not import credentials or history from other Codex applications.

Chart history contains sample times, quota windows, remaining percentages, reset times, and continuity markers. It contains no email address, account identifier, or authentication token. When recording new data, the app limits history to the most recent seven days and at most 20,000 entries. Old records are not automatically removed on a timer while the app is stopped.

The component also stores auxiliary files such as installation identifiers, state, caches, and local logs. The store version disables the main app’s development verification records; component logs may still remain locally. The app has no feature that uploads chart history or these local logs to the developer.

Clearing history and signing out

Clear History clears this app’s chart records only. A successful sign-out clears this app’s sign-in state and chart history; changing accounts also clears chart history. These actions do not erase all component caches or logs, sign you out of other Codex applications, or delete your OpenAI account or server-side records. Use OpenAI’s privacy and account-management channels for account or server-side data requests.

Demo, network status, and launch at login

Demo samples stay in memory. The live quota client pauses during the demo and resumes afterward. Status icons use the system’s network-path status without reading Wi-Fi names or browsing history or sending additional probe requests. Launch at login is an opt-in choice that you can turn off in the app menu.
